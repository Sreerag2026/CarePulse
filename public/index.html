How it works

From sign-up to live readings in three steps

  1. 1

    Register your child

    Create a parent account with your child's details. You'll get a Patient ID.

  2. 2

    The device sends readings

    The monitoring device sends heart rate, SpO₂, temperature and blood pressure to CarePulse.

  3. 3

    Parents and doctors see updates

    Parents follow their child's vitals. Doctors review every patient and download reports.

Security and privacy

Medical data deserves care

Here's how CarePulse protects the information you share with it.

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text

    They're hashed with bcrypt before they're saved.

  • Parents see only their own child

    Each parent account can open one patient's records and no one else's.

  • Doctor accounts need a registration code

    Only people with the hospital's code can create a doctor account.

  • Sign-ins expire automatically

    Every session is a signed token that expires after 12 hours.
